2. Where Your Chats Are Stored

  • Chat history is stored only on your device, not on our servers.
  • When you send a question, the question, part of the recent conversation, and your chart calculations pass through our server to an AI provider to generate the answer. Our server does not store them.
  • If you tap "Remember", a summary is stored on our server and used in later consultations.
  • Chats you report are stored on our server for review.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

6. Advertising

  • Free users see Google AdMob ads, which may be personalized using the advertising ID and app usage.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to turn off tracking, or decline the tracking prompt.
  • Ad rewards are granted whether or not you allow tracking.
  • Subscribers do not see banner ads. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one for Apples.
  • Turn off Settings → "Send usage statistics and crash reports" to stop Google Analytics and Crashlytics collection.

7. Automated Decisions

AI answers and daily fortunes are reference content and are not automated decisions that significantly affect your rights or obligations.

8. Your Rights

  • You may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent at any time.
  • In the app: edit birth details in Settings → Profile, delete registered people in relationship analysis, delete everything via Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • By email: z44446in@gmail.com. We respond within 10 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, Japan, or US states such as California), you may have additional rights, including data portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them. We do not sell personal information. Personalized advertising through AdMob can be turned off as described in Section 6.

9. Children

The Service is not for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn that we have, we delete it without delay.

10. Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest in the cloud
  • Database rules that let you read only your own data; all changes made on our server
  • AI and payment secret keys kept on the server, never in the app
  • App Check to block abusive calls
  • Data minimization: chat history is not stored on our servers
